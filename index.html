1. Introduction

Nexabytes Studio ("we", "us", or "our") built Tile Explore (package: com.nexabytes.tileexplore) as an ad-supported triple-match puzzle game. This Privacy Policy informs players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information when playing Tile Explore.

By installing or playing Tile Explore, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Game Features & Local Data Storage

Tile Explore is designed with privacy-by-design principles. We do not collect or store your private identifiable information on our servers:

  • Gameplay Progress & Scores: Your highest unlocked levels, coins, win streaks, booster inventories (undo, shuffle, hint), and theme preferences are stored exclusively on your local device storage.
  • Custom Tile Studio: The "My Tiles" feature allows you to upload custom images from your camera roll or files to customize puzzle tile faces. All uploaded custom tile images remain 100% locally in your device's browser/application storage. They are never transmitted, uploaded, or analyzed on any external server.
  • Local Data Reset: Players can wipe all stored levels, coins, and custom tiles at any time directly through the "Reset Game Progress" button inside the in-game Settings menu.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob SDK

Tile Explore uses the Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements (banner ads, interstitial ads after completed levels, and rewarded video ads for optional free coins or boosters).

Google AdMob Information: AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA or Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, operating system, and crash/diagnostic logs to serve relevant or non-personalized advertisements according to Google's policies.

For more details on how Google uses data when you use partner apps, please review Google's official privacy documentation:
Google Advertising Policies & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Offline Gameplay & Network State Monitoring

Tile Explore actively monitors the device's internet connectivity state. If your internet connection drops during gameplay, the game automatically pauses to protect your progress and game state. When your internet connection is restored, gameplay can seamlessly resume. No browsing history or unrelated network traffic is ever inspected or retained.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Tile Explore does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the app, please contact us at Ikbalsiddiki45@gmail.com, and we will take prompt action to resolve it.

6. User Data Rights & Opt-Out

You have complete control over your data in Tile Explore:

  • You can reset or delete all game progress anytime in Settings.
  • You can reset or personalize your Google Advertising ID in your device's operating system settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can choose whether to watch optional rewarded video ads.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We advise you to review this page occasionally for any changes. Any updates will be reflected directly in this document with a refreshed date.
